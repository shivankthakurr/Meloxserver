Privacy Policy

Melox · Last updated: September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Melox collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. Melox is developed and maintained by an independent developer.

Information We Collect

  • Username — the display name you choose is used within features like Listen Together, so other participants in a session can identify you. It is not tied to your real identity.
  • Session data — when you use Listen Together, temporary session identifiers and room codes are used to connect you with another device. This data lives only in server memory while a session is active and is not permanently stored.
  • Playback activity — track names and playback state (play/pause/position) are relayed between devices in the same Listen Together session so playback can stay in sync. This is not logged or retained after the session ends.
  • Chat messages — messages sent within a Listen Together session are relayed in real time between participants. Messages are not stored on the server and are not visible to anyone outside that session.

What We Don't Collect

  • We do not collect your name, phone number, email address, or precise location.
  • We do not sell or share your data with advertisers or third parties.
  • We do not run ads or ad-tracking SDKs inside the app.

Third-Party Content

Melox streams music from third-party sources. Your use of that content is also subject to those platforms' own terms of service. Melox does not control or store the media itself.

Data Storage

Listen Together runs on an in-memory server: room and session data exists only while a session is active and is automatically cleared afterward. No persistent chat history or listening history is stored on our servers.

Your Choices

  • You can change or clear your username at any time in Settings.
  • You can block other users within Listen Together, and manage blocked users from Settings.
  • Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored preferences from your device.

Children's Privacy

Melox is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated as features change. Continued use of the app after changes means you accept the updated policy.
